# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

OurCoordinates serves people shopping for a meaningful reminder of a place: partners marking where they met, families preserving a home or milestone, travelers remembering a destination, and gift-givers who want something more personal than an off-the-shelf piece. They need to understand the idea quickly, trust the personalization process, and feel confident that the finished jewelry will carry the right story.

## Product Purpose

OurCoordinates turns meaningful locations into personalized jewelry and keepsakes. The family-owned, online-only business began during COVID as a way to make ends meet, inspired by a family trip to Santorini, Greece, and the realization that a place can hold an entire chapter of someone's life. Success means helping customers translate those places into wearable reminders with a clear, dependable ordering experience.

## Brand Personality

Intimate, precise, understated. The voice should feel personal without becoming sentimental or theatrical, and confident without borrowing the language of generic luxury brands.

## Anti-references

- Generic luxury-jewelry language built around exclusivity, status, or vague claims of elegance.
- Long SEO-first copy that repeats the same idea without adding trust or meaning.
- Invented founder portraits, named artisans, statistics, timelines, or guarantees that the business cannot verify.
- Treating a photo proof as guaranteed; the team tries to provide one whenever possible, but it can occasionally be missed.
- Template-like card walls, excessive decorative labels, or an editorial aesthetic that overwhelms the products and customer stories.

## Design Principles

1. Lead with the human reason the business exists, then show how that purpose reaches the customer.
2. Make precision visible through coordinates, engraving details, and a clear process rather than unsupported superlatives.
3. Use real product and customer imagery to prove the story; never substitute generic jewelry theater.
4. Keep the path forward obvious with direct links to shop, understand the process, and find a meaningful place.
5. Say only what the business can consistently deliver, especially around production and photo proofs.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Target WCAG 2.1 AA contrast and keyboard usability. Preserve clear heading structure, descriptive image alt text, visible focus states, comfortable reading widths, and responsive layouts without horizontal overflow. Respect reduced-motion preferences and avoid relying on color or animation alone to communicate meaning.
